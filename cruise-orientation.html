Dear Guests, Bon Voyage!

We are getting ready for our upcoming Cruise to the Mediterranean on NCL Epic.
We hope you are getting ready as well. Below are some important reminders to ensure
a smooth and unforgettable journey at sea.

📋 DOCUMENTS

Please bring all necessary documents with you. Do not forget your passport. Double check that your passports are current and valid for at least 6 months past the date of sailing. If your citizenship requires a visa – please get one.

For more details, visit www.visahq.com

🛡️ TRAVEL INSURANCE

All cabins are equipped with personal safes. We will not be responsible for lost or stolen items. We strongly recommend purchasing adequate Medical, Travel & Luggage Insurance to safeguard against life's surprises. We cannot accept responsibility for any losses or expenses incurred due to lack of insurance.

⏰ EMBARKATION & DEPARTURE

Port Arrival: Please arrive at the San Juan Port for check-in beginning at 12:00 PM.

Check-in Closes: Late afternoon

Boarding Ends: Strictly at 5:00 PM – early arrival is strongly recommended

Ship Departure: 7:00 PM (check-in counters close 2 hours before)

Airport Transfer: The port is approximately 45 minutes from San Juan International Airport. Flights on embarkation day should land no later than 1:00 PM to allow sufficient time for baggage claim, transfer, and check-in.

🚌 TRANSFERS

Transfers are offered by Norwegian in coach bus:

  • One Way: $49.50 per person
  • Roundtrip: $99 per person
✅ ONLINE CHECK-IN

Available on www.ncl.com from 21 to 4 days before departure. This ensures smoother embarkation. If you don't check in online, bring your passport for port check-in.

🍽️ KOSHER DINING

All meals will take place in the "SPIEGEL TENT", our kosher restaurant and synagogue, located on Deck 6 FWD.

  • Breakfast & Lunch: Buffet style
  • Dinner: Plated meals with assigned seating

If you have dietary restrictions or want to sit with specific people at dinner, please inform your booking agent. We will do our best to accommodate you.

🕍 SYNAGOGUE & PRAYERS

All tefillot will take place in the "SPIEGEL TENT", our designated synagogue space on Deck 6 FWD.

🥪 LUNCH AT PORTS

While we dock at various ports, guests will take lunch with them from the ship. Please bring a backpack or similar bag to discreetly carry your lunch, as taking food off the ship is officially prohibited. If seen with food, security may not allow you to take it with you.
