## Frequently Asked Questions

### Travel Requirements

**What do I need to enter Panama?**
- Valid passport with at least six months of validity beyond your departure date
- Visa requirements vary by country; many don't need a visa for stays up to 180 days
- Proof of return or onward ticket may be required

### Accommodations

**What amenities does the hotel offer?**
- Social areas
- Swimming pool
- Fitness gym
- Free Wi-Fi
- Elevator and stairs access

**Are there separate pool hours for men and women?**
No, there are no separate hours for the pool.

### Kosher Dining & Facilities

**Are there kosher restaurants in Panama City?**
Yes! Panama City offers over 40 kosher restaurants and eateries. [See our complete list here](#).

**Are there synagogues in Panama City?**
Absolutely. You'll find several Sephardic synagogues and an Ashkenazi synagogue called Beth El.

**How do I identify kosher products in regular supermarkets?**
Look for kosher certification symbols from authorities like:
- Shevet Ahim
- OU (Orthodox Union)
- OK Kosher

**Can I find kosher wine in Panama City?**
Yes, kosher wine is available at:
- Super Kosher
- Deli K
- Soho Market
- The Winery

### Practical Information

**What is the official currency?**
Panama accepts both the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) and the U.S. Dollar.

**What should I pack?**
- Lightweight clothing
- Sunscreen
- Hat
- Raincoat
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Insect repellent
- All travel documents

**Is tipping customary?**
Tipping is appreciated but not mandatory. A 10-20% tip is common in restaurants and for tour guides.

**What language is spoken?**
Spanish is the official language, but English is widely spoken in tourist areas.

**What type of electrical outlets are used?**
Panama uses type A and type B electrical outlets—the same as the USA. No converter needed!

**What is the legal drinking age?**
The legal drinking age in Panama is 18 years old.
